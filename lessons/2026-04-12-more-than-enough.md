# Lesson Plan — April 12, 2026
## Song: More Than Enough (Mother's Day)
## Music Leader: Tucker Dansie

---

## Lesson Goal
Teach the children the song "More Than Enough" by Shawna Edwards in time for Mother's Day. Help them understand the meaning and be able to sing it with feeling.

---

## Materials Needed
- iPad with flipchart loaded: willowaymedia.vercel.app/flipcharts/more-than-enough/flipchart.html
- TV or screen to AirPlay to (optional)
- Small prizes for games (stickers, candy, etc.)

---

## Lesson Flow (~20 minutes)

### 1. Intro (2 min)
- Ask: "What does your mom do for you every day?"
- Let a few kids share
- Transition: "We're going to learn a song about moms and how much they do for us"

### 2. First Listen (3 min)
- Play the song once (YouTube or recording)
- Ask kids: "What was your favorite line?"

### 3. Teach with Flipchart (8 min)
**Round 1 — Full Lyrics mode**
- Go through all slides, sing each line together 2x

**Round 2 — Keywords mode**
- Cover the slides, show just the keywords
- Have kids fill in the gaps verbally

**Round 3 — Guess mode (Fill in the Blank)**
- Show slides with blanks
- Kids shout out the missing words

### 4. Games (5 min)

**Game 1 — Freeze Sing**
- Play/sing the song, when you stop playing they freeze
- Whoever is still singing the right word gets a point/treat

**Game 2 — Photo Only Challenge**
- Switch flipchart to 📷 Photo mode
- Show an image — kids race to say the correct lyric line
- First one to say it correctly wins a small treat

**Game 3 — Popcorn Singing**
- Point to a child mid-song to keep singing solo for a few words
- Then point to another — keeps everyone engaged

### 5. Close (2 min)
- Talk briefly about what the song means — moms show love every day
- "This will be our gift to our moms on Mother's Day"
- Sing through once more, full song
