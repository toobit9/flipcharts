Info and Tutorial

Use Open Flipchart for normal singing practice. Use FILL IT IN! when you want the guessing game: it shows a picture first, then a clue with one or more missing words, then the full answer.

Flipchart Buttons Back and Next move through the slides. The restart icon jumps back to the beginning, and the music-note icon returns to the song library.
Display Modes The top-right icon cycles through full lyrics, missing-word practice, keyword prompts, and photo-only mode.
Accompaniment Songs with music show small play/pause and stop controls near the top. The audio keeps playing while you flip slides.
FILL IT IN! Start the game from the song library. Press Next to move from picture, to clue, to answer, then on to another random slide.
